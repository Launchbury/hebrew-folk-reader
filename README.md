# Classic Folk Tales Hebrew Reader: Audio

These are the audio recordings of the five stories in *Classic Folk Tales Hebrew Reader*, by John Launchbury. Follow along in the book, or listen first and then read.

**Listen online: https://launchbury.github.io/hebrew-folk-reader/**

## The stories

1. Jack and the Beanstalk (ג'ק וגבעול השעועית)
2. The Fisherman and His Wife (הדייג ואשתו)
3. Rumpelstiltskin (רומפלשטילצכן)
4. Puss in Boots (החתול במגפיים)
5. Beauty and the Beast (היפה והחיה)

Each story can be played in the browser or downloaded as an MP3 from the page above. The MP3 files are also in the [`audio`](audio) folder of this repository.
